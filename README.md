# dhrms_sih

SIH 2025 PROJECT

## Getting Started

🏥 Digital Health Record Management System (DHRMS)

A mobile-first (Android & iOS) + smartwatch-enabled healthcare application designed for migHealth Data Entry & Record Management

Doctors upload test results, prescriptions, treatments.

Data linked to worker's UHI, synced securely (cloud + blockchain).

Hospital Administration & Doctor Management

Hospital administrators create and manage doctor profiles.

Doctor credentials and specializations maintained in hospital systems.

Hospital dashboards track patient count, doctor performance, and bed management.

Doctor Authentication & Dashboard Access

Doctors log in through hospital dashboard using secure credentials.

Access to individual doctor dashboard with patient management capabilities.

Role-based authentication ensuring proper access control.

Patient Registration & Management by Doctors

Doctors can register new patients if they don't have unique IDs.

Patient ID generation format: FIRSTNAME + 4 random digits.

Complete patient profile creation with medical history and contact information.

Prescription Management & Digital Healthcare

Doctors create digital prescriptions with detailed medication information.

Prescription tracking with dosage, duration, and status management.

Real-time prescription updates reflected in patient dashboard.

Patient Prescription Access

Patients view all prescriptions in their health dashboard.

Active and completed prescription tracking with doctor attribution.

Medication reminders and prescription status monitoring.workers in Kerala. The system ensures portable, secure, AI-driven health records and real-time outbreak prevention.

Built with Flutter (Dart) for the frontend and supported by a scalable, interoperable backend.

To provide accessible, secure, and intelligent healthcare management for migrant workers by integrating AI, IoT, blockchain, and telemedicine into one unified platform.

🔑 Confirmed Features
1. AI-Powered Disease Prediction & Early Warning

Machine learning models analyze health records to detect risks (TB, malaria, dengue, COVID).

Early alerts sent to healthcare authorities and local hospitals.

2. Universal Health Identity (UHI) with Unique ID

Every worker has a Unique Health ID = Short name + Last 4 digits of Aadhaar.

All medical data (from different hospitals/clinics) automatically linked to this ID.

3. Multi-Platform Accessibility

Available on Android & iOS (Flutter app).

Smartwatch support: vitals monitoring, alerts, and SOS functionality.

4. Proximity Infection Alert System

If an infected user comes near a healthy user:

Healthy user’s watch vibrates + phone alert shown.

Local hospital/area office notified automatically.

5. Telemedicine & Remote Consultation

Integrated video consultation for remote check-ups.

Reduces travel and ensures quick access to doctors.

6. Wearable & IoT Health Monitoring

Sync with smart bands/watches to track vitals (heart rate, oxygen, temp).

Detect fever clusters → trigger outbreak warning.

7. Emergency SOS & Health Hotlines

One-tap SOS button (on app & smartwatch).

Sends live location to ambulance, hospital, or NGOs.

Regional IVR helpline & WhatsApp chatbot for non-app users.

8. Insurance & Financial Inclusion

Direct integration with Ayushman Bharat & state schemes.

Migrants get cashless treatment at empaneled hospitals.

9. Employer Collaboration Portal

Employers (factories, construction firms):

Upload worker health check reports.

View compliance dashboards.

Receive alerts if outbreak risk detected in worker groups.

10. Data-Driven Health Intelligence

Government dashboards with:

Heatmaps of disease spread.

Predictive analytics for resource allocation.

Seasonal disease pattern detection.

11. Gamification for Health Awareness

Workers rewarded with points & badges for checkups, vaccinations, quizzes.

Encourages preventive care & active participation.

12. Privacy-First Security Layer

Role-based access (Worker, Doctor, Employer, Govt).

End-to-end encryption for all sensitive records.

Public dashboards → anonymized insights only.

13. Smartwatch Custom Alerts

Customizable vibration patterns for health alerts.

Emergency warnings pushed directly to the wrist.

14. Hospital Notification System

Automatic updates sent to local hospitals if:

A new infected case is detected.

A healthy user comes into contact with an infected user.

15. Unified Record Collection

All medical history organized under one UHI.

Supports multi-hospital data aggregation.

16. AI-Powered Chatbot

In-app AI health assistant for:

Symptom checking.

Appointment booking.

Health awareness tips in regional languages.(as of now only english)

17. Hospital-Doctor Management System

Comprehensive hospital administration with doctor management:

Hospital dashboards with doctor creation and management.

Doctor credential management and access control.

Patient count tracking and doctor performance metrics.

18. Doctor Portal & Authentication

Secure doctor login accessible only through hospital dashboard.

Individual doctor dashboards with patient management capabilities.

Role-based access ensuring doctors can only access through hospital authorization.

19. Advanced Patient Management by Doctors

Complete patient medical record viewing and management.

New patient registration with unique ID generation (FIRSTNAME + 4 digits).

Patient search, filtering, and detailed medical history access.

Doctor-patient relationship tracking and management.

20. Prescription Management System

Doctors can create, edit, and manage patient prescriptions.

Digital prescription creation with dosage, duration, and medication details.

Prescription history tracking and status management (Active/Completed).

Integration with patient health records for comprehensive care.

21. Patient Dashboard Prescription Integration

Users can view all prescriptions prescribed by doctors in their dashboard.

Active and completed prescription tracking with detailed medication information.

Prescription attribution showing prescribing doctor and date.

Full prescription management interface for patients.

22. Unified Healthcare Workflow

Complete hospital-doctor-patient ecosystem integration.

Seamless data flow from hospital administration to patient care.

Real-time prescription updates and medical record synchronization.

Comprehensive healthcare management from registration to prescription delivery.
